# HW1 Experiment Results (writeup source notes, not submission prose)

All numbers below were generated by the committed scripts; raw JSONL logs are
under `outputs/logs/` (gitignored) and plots under `outputs/figures/`.
Reported error bars are across independent seeds.

## Part 0

- MSP sanity (`dl_part0_sanity_msp.jsonl`): all 5 seeds recover every atom to
  `s_k>0.99` by one-based iteration 6--8 (budget 30); final minimum correlation
  is 0.9986--0.9993.
- ISTA warm-up (`dl_part0_warmup_ista.jsonl`): `f*=8814.0855168404`; entrywise
  support size 8273/64000 and support identification at t=2273; post-support
  geometric factor rho=0.99783. The curve is initially compatible with the
  O(1/k) upper bound, then becomes geometrically faster.
- Recovery convention: primary Eq. (3) max-correlation plus duplicate-excess
  count; Hungarian one-to-one mean also logged.

## Part 1

### T-axis (`dl_part1a_msp_convergence.jsonl`)

- D=16, theta=.1, N=8000, sigma=0, lambda=.6, 5 seeds, T=100.
- By about T=12 both metrics reach finite-sample / LASSO floors.
- Mean-curve recovery transient above floor: power alpha=4.307 (R2=.592),
  exponential rho=.350 (R2=.799). Per-seed alpha=5.970±2.161,
  rho=.210±.126.
- Mean-curve eval transient: power alpha=4.957 (R2=.608), exponential rho=.324
  (R2=.814). Per-seed alpha=5.653±2.142, rho=.257±.098.
- Interpretation: exponential is more consistent than power on the measured
  transient, but R2≈.8 and the short superlinear-to-floor trajectory make a
  single law inadequate; eval and recovery have different floors.

### N-axis (`dl_part1b_msp_sample_size.jsonl`)

- N={100,178,316,562,1000,1780,3160,5620,10000}, 5 seeds; all 45 runs reached
  stationarity, maximum T=126.
- Recovery: beta=1.285, R2=.936; per-seed beta=1.212±.192. N=10000 prediction
  relative error 16.1%.
- Eval excess: beta=.472, R2=.907; per-seed beta=.463±.077. At N=10000 the raw
  loss relative error is 13.9%, but the fitted quantity `E-E_inf` has 207%
  relative error. Thus the fit looks good in-range but does not support a
  stable eval-excess power law under extrapolation.

## Part 2 (`dl_part2_phase_transition.jsonl`)

- T*(theta), mean±std: .2: 8.0±2.55; .4: 9.8±1.79; .6: 17.8±1.30;
  .7: 26.6±8.17; .8: 48.0±4.85; .85: 88.0±16.51. No censored cost runs.
- Final recovered fraction mean±std: theta=.88: .9875±.028; .90: .9375±.088;
  .91: .4875±.277; .92: .175±.103; .93: .075±.052; .95 and 1.0: 0.
  Every transition run reached iterate stationarity before the 20000 cap.
- Held-out loss rises smoothly with density and has no sharp feature matching
  the recovery collapse near .90--.92: loss does not diagnose identifiability.
- Theory: E[z^2]=1, E[z^4]=3/theta, kappa=3(1/theta-1)>0; at theta=.91,
  kappa≈.297. At theta=1, for any orthogonal R, `Z'=ZR^T`, `A'=RA` gives the
  same X and Z' has the same Gaussian law. MSP exploits positive fourth-moment
  anisotropy; kappa→0 removes that signal.

## Part 3 (`dl_part3_{taxis,naxis}.jsonl`)

### PCA / whitening

- Full MNIST covariance: 80%, 90%, 99% variance need 44, 87, 331 directions.
- Chosen k=32. Relative Frobenius whitening covariance error: 2.77e-7.

### T-axis, N=12000, 400 blocks, 3 seeds

- m=64 final held-out loss 10.1889±.0098; random loss 15.9189±.3085;
  learned/random=.6402±.0128; code nnz fraction=.1305±.00084.
- m=128 final held-out loss 7.9752±.0238; random loss 11.8963±.0640;
  learned/random=.6704±.0043; code nnz fraction=.0740±.00040.
- Floor-aware held-out fits: m64 exponential rho=.9789 (R2=.954) vs power
  alpha=2.275 (R2=.841); m128 exponential rho=.9850 (R2=.822) vs power
  alpha=1.721 (R2=.774). Training-objective exponential R2=.938/.941.
- Interpretation: unlike MSP's very short transient, real-data overcomplete
  altmin has a long approximately geometric relaxation, followed by a floor.

### N-axis, 3 seeds

- 27/42 runs met the 1e-5/50-block criterion; 15 reached the allowed 1000-block
  cap (m64: 3 capped; m128: 12 capped). This limitation must be disclosed.
- m64 free-floor fit: beta=.558 (per-seed .555±.155), R2=.9999; N=12800
  prediction error 1.22%; final learned/random=.6408±.0129, nnz=.13063.
- m128: beta=.485 (per-seed .494±.184), R2=.9996; prediction error 1.54%;
  final learned/random=.6711±.0033, nnz=.07421.
- Synthetic eval beta=.472, so m128 is very similar and m64 modestly steeper;
  uncertainty across seeds is much larger than the mean difference.

### Atom interpretation

- Both dictionaries contain directly recognizable digit/stroke templates.
- m=128 contains more style variants and near-duplicates than m=64; increased
  overcompleteness improves held-out loss but lowers code density.

## Part 4 (`dl_part4_spectral_bias.jsonl`)

- Empirical Var(z_k) follows tau_k=c k^{-alpha}, validating that energy is not
  equalized. Expected sparsity sum p_k decreases from 2.079 (alpha=.75) to
  1.521 (1.0) to 1.175 (1.25).
- Alternating minimization, 80 atom-seed pairs per alpha:
  - alpha=.75: 80/80 recovered, t range 2--58;
  - alpha=1: 78/80, t range 1--79;
  - alpha=1.25: 72/80, t range 1--113.
  Higher energy atoms are generally earlier; separation and seed variability
  increase with alpha, and low-energy atoms account for failures.
- MSP at alpha=1: 80/80 recovered in 1--6 iterations. The pronounced altmin
  ordering largely disappears because MSP's polar projection updates the
  complete orthogonal basis jointly and enters a rapid local phase.
